# AGENTS.md - Workspace Rules for Hifzhly

## UI/UX & Design Guidelines (Anti-AI-Slop Policy)
- **Zero Emojis in UI**: Never use Unicode emojis (🔥, ⚠️, 🚀, ✨, 💡, 💯, 🎉, etc.) in components, buttons, navigation, headers, cards, or messages. Always use clean, professional SVG icons from `lucide-react` (shadcn standard).
- **No Excessive Badges**: Do not spam colorful rounded pill badges on every card or header. Maintain clean, restrained typography with subtle subheadings.
- **Icon Sizing & Consistency**: Use `lucide-react` with standard sizes (`w-4 h-4` for buttons, `w-5 h-5` for cards).
- **Tone & Aesthetics**: Serious, calm, scholarly, and khusyu' Islamic typography. High contrast, readable Arabic script (`font-quran text-3xl`+), and clean subtle borders.
